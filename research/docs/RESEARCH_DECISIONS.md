# Research decisions and unresolved tasks

1. **100 vs 102:** Phase 5/6 canonical 100 preserved unmodified as a provisional inventory. The Great Mosque of Djenné and Mosque of Amr ibn al-As are appended as `MOS-0101`, `MOS-0102`. Do not renumber existing IDs or confuse records with scholarly approval.
2. **Kairouan:** foundation in 670 is supported by Smarthistory's mosque discussion; UNESCO's city foundation alone would not have been sufficient. Present monument principally ninth century. UNESCO inscription relationship is within the Kairouan property.
3. **Córdoba:** original mosque 786–788; multiple early and later interventions; cathedral since 1236. 1984 UNESCO inscription of the mosque subsequently extended in 1994. Present structure has no single valid year.
4. **Isfahan:** date of the earliest mosque foundation remains non-exact in this limited evidence set. Do not assign c. 771 as a confirmed sorting year. Four-iwan and Seljuk dome claims are UNESCO-supported.
5. **Djenné:** original thirteenth-century foundation is a traditional account, not an exact historical fact. The present 1906–1907 reconstruction is strongly documented; the UNESCO inscription concerns Old Towns of Djenné, currently on List of World Heritage in Danger.
6. **Sultan Mosque:** National Museum collection record says rebuilding finished in 1928; National Library Board reports formal opening in 1929 (partly complete) and final completion in 1932. The possibility of different completion milestones is plausible but not yet conclusively resolved. Preserve both sources and the conflict.
7. **Amr ibn al-As:** Egyptian Ministry documents initial foundation 641, twentieth-century reconstruction. Source does not warrant a precise twentieth-century reconstruction year.
8. **Translations:** All 42 glossary entries and six trilingual summaries are consistent keys, but need native/editorial proofreading, especially proper names and technical Arabic/French.
9. **Photographs:** no asset is licensed/cleared in this package. Source rights differ from rights in actual photographs. Seek verifiable high-resolution front, rear, side, aerial, courtyard, interior, plan and section source assets.
10. **3D:** no GLB/glTF assets were provided; no visual authenticity guarantee is possible for unmeasured image-derived reconstruction.
11. **Measurements:** no architectural dimensions are published in the six records until measurement source, scope and methods are reconciled. Kairouan secondary dimensional figures previously conflicted.
12. **Chronological completeness:** the original 100 records have incomplete source and date validation. Do not bulk-promote them just because the JSON migration succeeds.
